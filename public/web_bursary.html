Financial Operations

Located within the Administration Block, the Bursar is the chief financial officer of G.U.S.S. Ringim. This office is responsible for the collection, documentation, and auditing of all fees and payments remitted by the student body and parent partners.

Student Fees & Levies

Manages the generation of invoices for school fees, PTA levies, and specialized club or laboratory practical dues.

Graduation Clearance

Provides the final financial clearance required before any Senior student can receive their SSCE certificates or digital RINOBA tokens.
