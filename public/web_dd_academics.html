Academic Portfolio

The Office of the Deputy Director II (Academic) is the intellectual core of G.U.S.S. Ringim. This office dictates the pedagogical direction of the institution, balancing abstract tasks like academic staff evaluation with the concrete management of the school's vast educational facilities.

Curriculum Matrix

Designs and enforces the dynamic class-subject routing for all Junior and Senior streams, ensuring strict compliance with national syllabus standards.

Staff Evaluation

Conducts the rigorous monitoring and evaluation of all teaching staff. Coordinates with Form Masters and H.O.Ds to review academic performance indices.

Academic Facilities

Manages the concrete academic assets of the school, including the Main Library and the specialized Science, E.I.M, and T.D Laboratories.

Examinations Office

Oversees the standalone Examination Complexes and coordinates internal terminal assessments, Mock qualifiers, WAEC, and NECO preparations.
