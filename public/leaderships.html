The Selection Protocol

The Trial Phase

As the outgoing SSS 3 students begin their national WAEC/NECO examinations, the administrative baton is temporarily passed to selected SSS 2 students. This "Acting Prefecture" phase allows the Directorate to actively monitor their leadership capabilities under pressure before confirming full appointment. These temporary assignments are one of the few exceptions allowed to be announced outside the standard Monday Assembly.

Cap Forfeiture Rule

Prefects are held to the absolute highest standard of discipline. Any verified incident of bullying junior students, academic malpractice, or violating the non-gadget policy results in immediate "Cap Forfeiture" (stripping of rank) by the Disciplinary Committee, leaving their position permanently blank on the hierarchy board until reassigned.

Executive Privileges

Head Student Exemptions

The Head Student is the only scholar granted the executive privilege to carry and operate a digital device/laptop publicly, specifically for the execution of official administrative documentation and liaison duties with the DD Administration.

Monday Assembly Command

During the mandatory Monday Assemblies, the Special Duty Prefect (the Auditor) and the Ameers hold the floor to issue student directives before the administrative staff takes over. The Special Duty Prefect ranks third in command, strictly monitoring the rest of the council.
