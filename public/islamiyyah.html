Theological Instruction

The Islamiyyah Section

An optional but highly prestigious weekend program cultivating religious discipline, knowledge, and character.

Program Framework

Operating deliberately on weekend evenings within the expansive Examination Halls, the Islamiyyah section offers students a profound opportunity to master Islamic principles. While enrollment is voluntary from any class level, completing the rigorous curriculum is required for students seeking the prestigious Double Honors at graduation.

Core Curriculum

  • Quranic Memorization
  • Hadith Studies
  • Principles of Fiqh

Leadership

The section is expertly managed by the Chief Imams and coordinated by the Ameer Prefects, ensuring high academic and moral standards.

The Grand Convocation

Students who successfully complete the Quranic recitation and pass all theological evaluations achieve the coveted "Double Honors" (SSCE Certificate + Islamic Certification).

This milestone is celebrated in a massive Convocation event hosted directly by the Director. Guests of honor regularly include the Emir of Ringim, traditional rulers, the Supreme Committee of Islamic Affairs, and families of the graduating scholars.
