Maintaining Order & Character

Disciplinary Committee

The central authority enforcing the strict rules and regulations of G.U.S.S. Ringim to forge a generation of highly disciplined leaders.

Home Disciplinary Committee

Command Structure

The Disciplinary Committee is the apex judicial body within the school. It is officially chaired by the Deputy Director I (Administration), who reviews all major violations and enforces institutional mandates.

The Strict Non-Gadget Policy

According to school regulations, it is strictly prohibited to use any type of smart gadgets including smartphones, laptops, and tablets. Exceptions are only made for approved scientific calculators (e.g., KAPEC, CASIO).

Enforcement Protocol

If caught with a contraband device, a student faces severe and immediate penalties. The most critical sanction requires the student to personally destroy the device with a hammer, manually chew the SIM/Memory cards to pieces, and receive 12 mandatory lashes.

Exceptions & Executive Privileges

Head Student

As the supreme student head, the Head Student is legally permitted to use digital devices (including laptops) publicly to execute administrative duties on behalf of the management.

Emergency Use

Health Prefects (for critical medical coordination) and House Captains (for severe hostel emergencies) are permitted restricted use of phones strictly within the Area Hostels.

GEPSU Protocols

The 19 Northern States Exchange Programme introduces unique disciplinary pathways.

State Patrons

Each exchange state micro-union (e.g., KASEPSA, GOSSA) is assigned a faculty Patron. This patron acts as a direct advocate to prevent bullying and ensure fair disciplinary hearings.

Sanction Limits

To protect the diplomatic integrity of the exchange program, Exchange Students are treated exceptionally well. An Exchange Student can generally only be suspended if their violation is severe enough to trigger an outright dismissal for a non-exchange student.

Prefect Limitations

While Prefects wield immense power over Junior students (JSS1 - SSS2), they are strictly forbidden from disciplining a fellow Senior (SSS3). All Senior infractions must be reported to the Head Student or the Disciplinary Committee.
