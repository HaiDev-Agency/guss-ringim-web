The Demonstration Subsidiary

Situated securely inside the main campus behind the E.I.M laboratories, the Unique Nursery & Primary School operates as a specialized demonstration subsidiary. Originally established to cater to the younger children of staff members, it now provides world-class foundational education to the wider Ringim community.

Fully Secured Perimeter

The primary section is completely fenced off from the main secondary school premises, ensuring absolute safety and an age-appropriate environment for young learners.

Dedicated Administration

While answerable to the apex Director of G.U.S.S., the primary school features its own dedicated Head Master and highly trained elementary staff.

Cognitive Growth

Equipping the next generation with advanced literacy, numeracy, and creative problem-solving skills.
