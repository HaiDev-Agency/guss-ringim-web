Campus Geography

A fully enclosed, university-scale ecosystem divided into distinct functional quadrants.

Quadrant 1

The Area Classes

The academic engine of the school. This vast quadrant houses the main student classrooms, the Administrative block, specialized laboratories, the Main Library, Examination Halls, and the fenced Unique Nursery & Primary School[cite: 6].

Explore Laboratories
Quadrant 2

The Area Hostels

A highly secure residential zone featuring 19 independent dormitories (such as Aminu Yahaya and Dutse House). It includes the central Kitchen & Dining hall, the main Student Clinic, public latrines, and the Grand Mosque for daily congregational prayers[cite: 6].

Quadrant 3

Athletics & Gardens

A massive physical buffer zone dividing the academic/boarding areas from the staff quarters. It hosts the Main Football Pitch, Basketball grids, Volleyball courts, and vast agricultural gardens yielding fresh fruits[cite: 6].

Quadrant 4

The Staff Quarters

The residential enclave for faculty and administration. Completely separated from the student areas by the vast sports landmass. It is strictly coordinated by the Quarter Masters and Quarter Prefects to ensure absolute privacy and prevent student trespassing[cite: 6].
