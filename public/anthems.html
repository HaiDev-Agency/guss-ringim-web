The Sovereign Anthem

So great, so mighty,
Far and wide, Ringim,
We come unity, uniting us.
Blending our diversity,
So blessed and versatile,
Nineteen states stand as one,
Honor, respect, binding us.
Supporting our fraternity,
Unity towards above,
Over the rest, behold!
The best in character,
Learning, discipline,
We rank higher than all the rest.
God bless us in Unity,
God bless us in Jigawa,
God bless our fatherland, Nigeria.

“Learning, Discipline, and Character”

Federal Republic of Nigeria

The National Anthem

"Nigeria, We Hail Thee"


Nigeria, we hail thee,
Our own dear native land,
Though tribe and tongue may differ,
In brotherhood we stand,
Nigerians all, are proud to serve
Our sovereign Motherland.
Our flag shall be a symbol
That truth and justice reign,
In peace or battle honour'd,
And this we count as gain,
To hand on to our children
A banner without stain.
O God of all creation,
Grant this our one request,
Help us to build a nation
Where no man is oppressed,
And so with peace and plenty
Nigeria may be blessed.

“Unity and Faith, Peace and Progress”

National Motto
