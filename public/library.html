The Academic Archive

The Main Library is a massive, meticulously organized facility designed for absolute academic focus. It holds thousands of volumes ranging from foundational JSS literature to advanced SSS Science, Arts, and Commercial textbooks. It provides the silent atmosphere necessary for students preparing for crucial WAEC and NECO examinations.

Extensive Catalogs

Aisles separated strictly by departmental routing to ensure students can rapidly find relevant academic material.

Silent Study Zones

Spacious reading tables where absolute silence is enforced by the Library Prefects to maximize concentration.

Borrowing & Lending Privileges

Students are actively encouraged to borrow textbooks and literature for evening study in the Area Hostels. The borrowing system is highly organized—students must present their official school ID to the Chief Librarian. A physical borrow card is issued and stamped, mandating a strict return date to ensure book availability for all scholars.
