Operational Scope

Situated directly adjacent to the Director's office, the Office of the Deputy Director I (Administration) is the logistical engine of G.U.S.S. Ringim. This office is responsible for the day-to-day coordination of non-academic staff, the oversight of the 19 Area Hostels, and the enforcement of the school's rigorous behavioral standards.

Disciplinary Chairman

Chairs the Disciplinary Committee, directly handling major infractions, reviewing House Master reports, and executing sanctions in line with institutional policy.

Hostel & Welfare Logistics

Receives recommendations from and supervises all House Masters. Oversees the Kitchen/Dining masters and ensures 5,000+ scholars are properly accommodated.

Campus Security

Coordinates with the security personnel at the main gates and manages the Quarter Masters to ensure students do not trespass into Staff Quarters.

Staff Duties

Monitors and assigns duties to non-academic staff, including the Sanitation Masters, Labor Masters, and school clinic personnel.
