Extra-Curricular Excellence

Clubs & Societies

Voluntary organizations fostering creativity, science, and cultural heritage under the guidance of active faculty patrons.

Most Active Society

Hausa Drama Club

The vibrant social heartbeat of the school. The Hausa Drama Club is officially the most active organization on campus, renowned for coordinating massive, deeply traditional events such as Hawan Kara and Hawan Daushe.

Faculty & Exco Synergy

The club's overwhelming success is driven by highly active Patrons who literally write the scripts and coordinate the stage plays. Furthermore, the student Executives (Excos) wield significant influence, directly recommending casting posts and specific cultural roles for participants during these major festivals.

Press Club

The voice of the students. The Press Club commands a standard, constant window during every Monday Assembly. Because assemblies only happen once a week, the Press Club is highly relied upon to deliver compiled campus news, global current affairs, and verified announcements before the official dismissal.

J.E.T.S Club

Junior Engineers, Technicians and Scientists. A highly intellectual society where students prototype inventions, prepare for national Olympiads, and utilize the specialized laboratories under expert Patron guidance.

CITAD Hub

Operating from the Computer Lab, this tech-focused society introduces students to digital literacy, data processing concepts, and technological advocacy, preparing them for a digital future.

Mathematics Club

A society dedicated to breaking the phobia of numbers. Members engage in advanced calculus prep, logical problem-solving debates, and inter-school math competitions.

Arabic Club

Focused on linguistic mastery, poetry, and debate in the Arabic language. It highly complements the efforts of the weekend Islamiyyah program and is a point of pride during cultural events.
