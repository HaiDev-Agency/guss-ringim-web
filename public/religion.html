Faith & Morality

Religious Activities

Fostering deep moral character, mutual respect, and spiritual devotion across all faiths.

M.S.S.N

Muslim Students Society of Nigeria

Under the supreme guidance of the Chief Imams and coordinated by the Ameer Prefects, the MSSN manages the daily Khams Salawat congregational prayers within the Hostel Mosques. They serve as the moral compass of the school, resolving student disputes through Islamic principles and directly facilitating the esteemed weekend Islamiyyah program.

Christian Students Union

Fellowship & Community

Operating with absolute freedom and structure, the Christian Students Union conducts daily devotions at specialized campus locations assigned by their President/Chairman. The union’s student leadership resolves internal peer issues and escalates severe concerns directly to their assigned Patrons (Christian Teachers).

The Sunday Outing Privilege

While the general student body only receives outings on Fridays, Christian scholars are granted a highly respected second outing day. Every Sunday morning, under the escort of their Patrons, they are permitted to leave the campus to attend main town churches in Ringim for worship and major religious events, returning in the afternoon.
