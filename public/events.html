Weekly Mandates & Routines

The strict structural schedules that maintain discipline and order on campus.

Labor Day

Every Wednesday
A mandatory physical routine for all Junior students (JSS 1 - SSS 2). The Labor Masters and Labor Prefects assign specific partitions of the campus for thorough cleaning, ensuring the school environment remains immaculate. Evading labor day attracts strict penalties from the Disciplinary Committee.

Friday Outings & Juma'at

Every Friday
The official school holiday and outing day. The Director hosts the morning assembly. At 10:00 AM, the gates open for the Ringim community to attend Juma'at prayers, and students may choose to pray on campus or at town mosques. It is a day of spiritual reflection and community integration.
