The 19 Area Hostels

G.U.S.S. Ringim operates a massive, highly structured residential quadrant. Scholars are distributed across 19 distinct dormitories—including prominent houses like Aminu Yahaya, Dutse House, and Prof. Ruqayyatu. Each house operates independently to foster localized brotherhood and fierce loyalty during Inter-House sports.

Chain of Command

Hostels are strictly governed by faculty House Masters who oversee general welfare and discipline. They are supported internally by student House Captains who act as the first line of leadership during evening prep and lights-out.

Provisions & Fire Hazards

Because the campus features a massive central Kitchen serving 3 square meals daily, cooking and the use of naked flames are strictly forbidden within the dormitories to prevent fire hazards. Students are only permitted to bring "dry provisions" such as cabin biscuits, garri, and milk.
