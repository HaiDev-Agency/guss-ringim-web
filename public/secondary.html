Junior Secondary (JSS 1-3)

The foundational sets building interdisciplinary knowledge.

The Junior section comprises JSS 1 to JSS 3. To maintain an optimal student-to-teacher ratio, each class level is divided into multiple Sets (e.g., JSS 1A, JSS 1B, up to JSS 1E).

Students in this section are exposed to a broad spectrum of exactly 9 fundamental subjects, laying the groundwork for their eventual specialization in the Senior tier.

Senior Secondary (SSS 1-3)

Highly specialized departmental routing and practical application.

The Senior section represents the pinnacle of academic routing. The population is divided across expansive sets (SSS A through H). Every student, regardless of department, undertakes the mandatory General Core Subjects: English, Mathematics, Civic Education, and IRS.

Departmental Subjects

Students routed to the Science department must undertake the core triplet of Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, conducted in our specialized laboratories.
