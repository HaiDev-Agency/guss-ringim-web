Student Unions

The geopolitical and religious assemblies representing the diverse 19 Northern States scholar demographics.

Geopolitical Assemblies

G.E.P.S.U

General Exchange Programme Students Union

The central umbrella representing scholars from the 17 non-host Northern States. Because the campus is highly diverse, individual states maintain micro-unions (like KASEPSA, GOSSA). These micro-unions elect Presidents who then represent their states on the central GEPSU board.

Guided by assigned Faculty Patrons to ensure diplomatic fairness.

J.I.K.A.S.U

Jigawa-Kano Students Union

As Jigawa and Kano provide the largest demographic block on campus, JIKASU serves as their unified executive body. To ensure fair representation down to the grassroots level, JIKASU operates mini sub-cabinets that organize students based on their Local Government Areas.

Works synergistically with GEPSU to organize cultural festivals.

Religious Societies

M.S.S.N

Muslim Students Society of Nigeria

The central society for Muslim scholars on campus. Coordinated by the Ameer Prefects and the Chief Imams, they manage daily prayers and oversee the integration of the weekend Islamiyyah program. They serve as the moral compass of the school, resolving student disputes through Islamic principles.

Christian Students Union

Fellowship & Community

Governed by their own elected Chairman and Secretary General, this union facilitates the religious beliefs of our Christian scholars. They conduct daily devotionals at designated campus locations. The union’s student leadership resolves internal peer issues and escalates severe concerns directly to their assigned Patrons (Christian Teachers).

The Sunday Outing Privilege

While the general student body only receives outings on Fridays, Christian scholars are granted a highly respected second outing day. Every Sunday morning, under the escort of their Patrons, they are permitted to leave campus to attend main town churches in Ringim for worship and major religious events, returning in the afternoon.
