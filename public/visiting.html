Time & Order

Visiting & Outings

The official schedules for town excursions, parent visits, and the strict administrative timelines of the campus.

Friday Outing Protocol

Fridays serve as the official outing day for the general student body. Classes conclude at a standard early time, allowing students to access Ringim town to purchase provisions, attend Juma'at prayers, and meet with visiting parents at the designated pavilions.

The Strict 5:00 PM Curfew

Time management is an absolute mandate at G.U.S.S. Ringim. On outing days, every student must return to the campus strictly before 5:00 PM.

When the clock strikes 5:00 PM, all auxiliary perimeter gates are heavily chained and locked. Only the Main Entry Gate remains active, supervised by the official Gatekeepers and Quarter Prefects. Any student caught arriving after 5:00 PM faces immediate disciplinary punishment for insubordination.

The Monday Assembly

Information flow is highly centralized. The Assembly is strictly a once-a-week event held only on Mondays.

Whatever events or administrative decisions occur during the week, they must wait until the following Monday to be delivered to the general student body by the Director or the Press Club.

The Only Exceptions

  • The announcement of Acting Prefects during WAEC.
  • The official swearing-in of the new Real Prefecture.
  • Emergency holiday briefings (e.g., duration of break, resumption items).
