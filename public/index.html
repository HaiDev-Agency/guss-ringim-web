Unique Nursery & Primary

A self-contained, model demonstration school operating safely within the campus grounds behind the E.I.M laboratories. We lay the foundational blocks of literacy, numeracy, and cognitive development in a highly secure, child-friendly environment.

Structured strictly into the Junior Section (JSS 1-3) and Senior Section (SSS 1-3). The Senior curriculum branches into highly specialized departments with dedicated, standalone laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Technical Drawing (T.D), and Electrical Installation (E.I.M).

Islamiyyah Section

A rigorous, structured theological program operating on weekend evenings. Students focus on Quranic memorization, Tajweed, Hadith, and Fiqh, utilizing our high-capacity examination halls. Upon successful completion, scholars celebrate in a grand graduation ceremony hosted by the Emir of Ringim.

19 Area Hostels

Each hostel block is governed by dedicated House Masters and House Captains. The boarding area features its own Grand Mosque for Khams Salawat (daily prayers) and public facilities supervised closely by Sanitation Prefects.

Kitchen & Dining

A massive complex serving 3 square meals daily, firmly monitored by Kitchen Masters and Prefects. The campus also houses a dedicated School Clinic Sub-Unit for immediate medical attention under the Health Master.

Sports & Labor

Features a Main Football Stadium, Basketball courts, and Volleyball pitches. Students engage in mandatory Wednesday Labor Days and maintain vast school farmlands yielding fresh fruits managed by Garden Masters.

Student Union Hierarchy

Student governance mirrors high-level legislative assemblies. From the Head Student down to Club Executives, leadership is cultivated through structured, independent unions.

GEPSU

General Exchange Programme Students Union. Represents boarding scholars from the 17 non-host Northern States. Features local sub-unions (e.g. KASEPSA, GOSSA, KASSU, BOSSU) guided by State Patrons.

JIKASU

Jigawa-Kano Students Union. The unified executive body representing the host state and Kano. Operates mini sub-cabinets for precise local government area representation.
