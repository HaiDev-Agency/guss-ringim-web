Health & Nutrition

Wellbeing & Medical Care

Ensuring the physical health, rapid medical response, and strict nutritional welfare of our 5,000+ boarding scholars.

The School Clinic

Operating around the clock within the Area Hostels quadrant, the School Clinic is the immediate first-responder unit for all scholar health concerns. It is rigorously managed by the faculty Health Masters and actively assisted by the student Health Prefects, who are granted emergency campus movement privileges.

The Referral Protocol

The campus clinic is fully equipped for first aid, routine fever management, and minor injuries. However, the school operates a strict, zero-risk medical protocol. Any condition deemed critical or requiring specialized diagnostic testing is immediately routed to the Ringim General Hospital. The DD Administration directly coordinates with parents in these severe instances to ensure full transparency and immediate care.

Nutritional Matrix

Proper nutrition is the fuel for academic excellence. Because personal cooking is strictly banned to prevent fire hazards, the massive Central Kitchen handles the dietary needs of the entire student body through an unyielding daily schedule.
