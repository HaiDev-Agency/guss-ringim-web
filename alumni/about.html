About RINOBA

Understanding the core mission, vision, and operational mandate of the Ringim Old Boys Association.

Our Mission

To cultivate a powerful, lifelong global network among the alumni of Government Unity Secondary School, Ringim. We aim to actively facilitate professional mentorship, provide unwavering support to our Alma Mater, and uphold the school's founding principles of discipline, academic rigor, and cross-cultural unity.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the most impactful, structurally organized, and resource-rich alumni association in Nigeria. A network where the legacy of the Northern States Exchange Programme continues to thrive, driving national integration and empowering the next generation of global leaders.

The Core Mandate

G.U.S.S. Ringim was founded on the ideology of integration. By bringing young minds from 19 different Northern states to live, study, and govern together, it forged unbreakable bonds.

The primary mandate of RINOBA is to ensure those bonds never sever after graduation. Our Central Executives are tasked with maintaining a comprehensive, digitized directory of all members. We are mandated to intervene in infrastructural decay, provide supplementary funding to the school's administration through our Endowment Fund, and serve as an external advisory board to the Directorate.

Once a Ringim Scholar, Forever a Brother.

The Ringim Old Boys Association (RINOBA) is the official unified body of all graduates from Government Unity Secondary School, Ringim. We are dedicated to bridging the generational gap, upholding the rich legacy of the Northern States Exchange Programme, and actively driving the infrastructural and academic advancement of our beloved Alma Mater.
