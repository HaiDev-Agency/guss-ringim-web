The Association Pillars

Global Networking

Leverage a vast directory of professionals spanning across the 19 Northern states and globally. Connect, collaborate, and build powerful strategic partnerships.

Generational Mentorship

Senior alumni actively guide recent graduates and current scholars. Through RINOBA, we ensure the high moral and academic standards of G.U.S.S. are passed down.

Alma Mater Support

Giving back is our core mandate. We coordinate funding for laboratory upgrades, student scholarships, and infrastructural development at the Ringim campus.

The Central Database

RINOBA is more than an association; it is a lifeline. We are building a massive, highly secured central database to connect graduates dating back to the 1980s. Whether you are looking for long-lost brothers, seeking career opportunities, or offering mentorship to younger sets, the digital directory is your ultimate tool.

The Ringim Old Boys Association (RINOBA) is the official unified body of all graduates from Government Unity Secondary School, Ringim. We are dedicated to bridging the generational gap, upholding the rich legacy of the Northern States Exchange Programme, and actively driving the infrastructural and academic advancement of our beloved Alma Mater.
